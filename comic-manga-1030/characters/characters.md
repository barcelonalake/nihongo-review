# Character Definitions - 一緒に行きませんか（日語課漫畫版）

**Style**: baoyu-comic `--art manga --tone warm` (+ ohmsha educational: student/mentor, visual metaphors, no talking-heads)
**Art Direction**: Authentic Japanese manga (日漫) — clean black ink lines, screentone shading, light warm color accents, speed lines, emotion marks (汗滴・！・？), expressive anime faces, right-to-left panel reading, Japanese speech bubbles, SFX
**Consistency phrase**: Japanese educational slice-of-life manga, black ink linework with screentone, soft warm skin tones, large expressive eyes, speech bubbles with Japanese text, speed lines and emotion symbols, Tokyo everyday settings, right-to-left panel flow, warm golden lighting

## Character 1: ハルト (Hart / protagonist)

**Role**: Protagonist — adult language-school student, newcomer to Tokyo
**Age**: late 30s (~37)
**Residence**: Kagurazaka (神楽坂), Tokyo
**School**: Japanese language school in Chiyoda area
**Name note**: fictional; NEVER 呂 / りょ / real user likeness

**Appearance**:
- Face: oval, soft jaw, slight laugh lines (adult, not teen)
- Hair: short black hair with a few silver strands at temples, neat side part
- Eyes: medium-large friendly brown eyes, educational-manga style (approachable, not sparkly-shoujo)
- Build: average height, slim-average adult male
- Distinguishing: thin rectangular glasses, small notebook always nearby

**Costume**:
- Default: navy casual blazer over cream knit sweater, dark chinos, brown loafers, canvas tote with language textbook
- Color palette: navy #2C3E50, cream #F5E6D3, warm brown #8B6F47
- Accessories: rectangular glasses, tote bag, map/phone

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral: gentle focus, slight smile
- Happy/Excited: bright eyes, open mouth smile, sparkles
- Thinking/Confused: furrowed brow, sweat drop, hand on chin
- Determined: jaw set, fist clenched lightly

## Character 2: ミキ先生 (Teacher Miki)

**Role**: Mentor / friendly language-school teacher
**Age**: early 40s

**Appearance**:
- Face: round-oval, warm smile
- Hair: shoulder-length dark brown hair, soft waves, often tucked behind one ear
- Eyes: kind almond eyes, soft
- Build: medium height, approachable
- Distinguishing: small cherry-blossom enamel pin on collar

**Costume**:
- Default: soft sage-green cardigan over white blouse, beige skirt or slacks, low heels
- Color palette: sage #68A67D, white, beige #D4C4A8, cherry accent #E07A5F
- Accessories: sakura pin, whiteboard marker, grammar notebook

**Expression Range**:
- Neutral: calm encouraging smile
- Happy: warm laugh, eyes crescent
- Teaching: pointing gesture, "aha" sparkle
- Gentle correction: soft head tilt, kind eyes (never scolding)
